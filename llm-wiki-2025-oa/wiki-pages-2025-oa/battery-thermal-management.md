# Battery Thermal Management

**Summary:** In the 2025 open-access corpus, battery thermal management (BTMS) is studied almost exclusively through own pack- or module-level experiments on commercial cylindrical and pouch cells, comparing air, phase change material (PCM), liquid, immersion and passive metal-foam cooling by maximum temperature (Tmax) and cell-to-cell temperature difference (ΔT). Modelling, where present, is mostly lumped heat balance; only two studies use spatially resolved 3D models validated against own data.

## Synthesized Knowledge

**Cooling concepts and headline results.** Air cooling serves as the baseline in several studies. A fan-assisted 4S2P module of Panasonic-Sanyo NCR18650GA cells with a honeycomb perforated plate reduced Tmax by 38.82% at 12 A and 28.89% at 16 A relative to no cooling (Source: [[a-study-on-the-removal-of-heat-generated-by-a-lithium-ion-battery-modu]]). Hybrid PCM–air concepts were tested on a 3S3P pack of Sony-Murata US18650VTC5 cells, where RT47 PCM with copper fins cut the maximum temperature difference by about 39–62% versus air cooling and kept Tmax at 55 °C at 3C and 3 m/s (Source: [[a-novel-hybrid-cooling-system-for-a-lithium-ion-battery-pack-based-on]]), and on a nine-cell series pack of Samsung SDI INR18650-25R cells, where 40–42 °C paraffin kept the maximum surface temperature below 42 °C while the air-only baseline rose from 38 °C to 55 °C across 22–42 °C ambient at 3C (Source: [[experimental-investigation-of-phase-change-material-based-battery-pack]]). For NCR18650GA cells in RT42/expanded-graphite blocks, an electrolytic Ni-P-Cu coating raised thermal conductivity to 27.1 W/m·K and lowered the 2.5C peak from approx. 61 °C (uncoated) to approx. 53 °C while extending discharge from approx. 900 s to 1400 s (Source: [[electrolytic-ni-p-and-ni-p-cu-coatings-on-pcm-loaded-expanded-graphite]]). Liquid cooling of a 3s2p NCR18650GA module showed that the cooling time constant scales inversely with thermal-interface-material diffusivity, with 40% BN–TPU best (Source: [[polymer-bn-composites-as-thermal-interface-materials-for-lithium-ion-b]]). Oil immersion (Therminol D-12, 0.8 LPM) of a 5S7P Samsung SDI INR21700-48X module gave Tmax/ΔT of 38.6 °C/4.3 °C at 3.0C charge and 43.0 °C/5.5 °C at 3.0C discharge (Source: [[experimental-study-on-thermal-management-of-5s7p-battery-module-with-i]]). Two-phase immersion in Novec 7000 preheated to 33 °C limited terminal-to-fluid temperature differences of an A123 Systems ANR26650M1-B LFP cell to two to three times lower values than single-phase natural convection (Source: [[thermal-performance-of-lithium-ion-battery-tabs-under-liquid-immersion]]). A passive Al-foam frame lowered the stabilized temperature increment of a two-cell US18650VTC6 pack by 20%, and 46% with an added Peltier cell (Source: [[comparative-analysis-of-innovative-cooling-systems-for-li-ion-cells-us]]).

**Boundary conditions and temperature sensing.** Ambient control varies strongly: a controlled room at 35 ± 1 °C (Source: [[a-novel-hybrid-cooling-system-for-a-lithium-ion-battery-pack-based-on]]), a thermal chamber at 22–42 °C with ±0.5 °C accuracy (Source: [[experimental-investigation-of-phase-change-material-based-battery-pack]]), conditioned chambers at 25 °C (Source: [[electrolytic-ni-p-and-ni-p-cu-coatings-on-pcm-loaded-expanded-graphite]]; [[experimental-study-on-thermal-management-of-5s7p-battery-module-with-i]]), only "approximately 25 °C" at test start without a stated control method (Source: [[a-study-on-the-removal-of-heat-generated-by-a-lithium-ion-battery-modu]]), a sealed environmental chamber whose temperature is not reported (Source: [[polymer-bn-composites-as-thermal-interface-materials-for-lithium-ion-b]]), and no reported ambient value at all (Source: [[comparative-analysis-of-innovative-cooling-systems-for-li-ion-cells-us]]; [[experimental-study-of-pouch-type-battery-cell-thermal-characteristics]]). Mechanical constraint is never specified as a pressure: fixtures are cell holders with 26.5 mm spacing (Source: [[a-study-on-the-removal-of-heat-generated-by-a-lithium-ion-battery-modu]]), 3D-printed supports (Source: [[electrolytic-ni-p-and-ni-p-cu-coatings-on-pcm-loaded-expanded-graphite]]), an aluminium box with 2 mm gaps (Source: [[experimental-study-on-thermal-management-of-5s7p-battery-module-with-i]]) or an aluminium manifold with 1.4 mm TIM slabs (Source: [[polymer-bn-composites-as-thermal-interface-materials-for-lithium-ion-b]]). Cell temperature is sensed with surface thermocouples (K- or T-type, 5–9 per pack) in almost all studies, with sampling rates often unreported; exceptions add infrared thermography (Source: [[comparative-analysis-of-innovative-cooling-systems-for-li-ion-cells-us]]), heat flux sensors at eight face positions on the 57 Ah Kokam SLPB120216216G1H pouch cell (Source: [[experimental-study-of-pouch-type-battery-cell-thermal-characteristics]]), and terminal-mounted adhesive thermocouples (Source: [[thermal-performance-of-lithium-ion-battery-tabs-under-liquid-immersion]]).

**Temperature-dependent performance and spatial inhomogeneity.** Only two studies report electrical performance versus temperature: on the ANR26650M1-B, preheating to 33 °C extended 10C discharge from 330 s to 341 s and cut 4C charging time by 31% (1777 s to 1233 s) (Source: [[thermal-performance-of-lithium-ion-battery-tabs-under-liquid-immersion]]), and on NCR18650GA the better-cooled pack discharged longer at 25 °C ambient (Source: [[electrolytic-ni-p-and-ni-p-cu-coatings-on-pcm-loaded-expanded-graphite]]). No study in this set reports temperature-dependent aging. Tab/terminal regions emerge as hot spots: the Kokam pouch cell is hottest at the tab–body junction with a top-to-bottom face gradient up to about 4 °C and exceeds 35 °C from about 1.35C without cooling (Source: [[experimental-study-of-pouch-type-battery-cell-thermal-characteristics]]).

**Models.** Model types range from lumped pack energy balances with Bernardi-type heat generation (Source: [[a-novel-hybrid-cooling-system-for-a-lithium-ion-battery-pack-based-on]]) and a 0-D electro-thermal model calibrated on a non-target flat cell (Source: [[comparative-analysis-of-innovative-cooling-systems-for-li-ion-cells-us]]) to a 3D NTGK model validated against measured Tmax at 0.5–2C with 4.54% average deviation, but only simulated at 4–5C (Source: [[experimental-study-of-pouch-type-battery-cell-thermal-characteristics]]), and a 3D COMSOL model that uses built-in LMO–graphite chemistry normalised to the NCA cell and is explicitly comparative, not voltage-predictive (Source: [[polymer-bn-composites-as-thermal-interface-materials-for-lithium-ion-b]]). The VTC5 study only compares its temperature rise with literature, with 13–49% deviations (Source: [[a-novel-hybrid-cooling-system-for-a-lithium-ion-battery-pack-based-on]]).

**Contradictions and inconsistencies.** Papers use different uniformity and temperature criteria: a 5 °C cell-to-cell ΔT limit (Source: [[a-study-on-the-removal-of-heat-generated-by-a-lithium-ion-battery-modu]]; [[experimental-study-on-thermal-management-of-5s7p-battery-module-with-i]]) versus a 2 °C degradation threshold together with a 40 °C limit (Source: [[thermal-performance-of-lithium-ion-battery-tabs-under-liquid-immersion]]), a 25–40 °C Tmax target (Source: [[experimental-study-on-thermal-management-of-5s7p-battery-module-with-i]]) and a 35 °C face threshold (Source: [[experimental-study-of-pouch-type-battery-cell-thermal-characteristics]]). The immersion-cooled INR21700-48X module itself exceeds its own 5 °C ΔT target at 3.0C discharge (5.5 °C) (Source: [[experimental-study-on-thermal-management-of-5s7p-battery-module-with-i]]). The NCR18650GA PCM/EG study states discharge rates of 1.25C and 2.5C but currents of 8.1 A and 10.2 A in its methods for a 6700 mAh pack, which are not mutually consistent as reported (Source: [[electrolytic-ni-p-and-ni-p-cu-coatings-on-pcm-loaded-expanded-graphite]]).
