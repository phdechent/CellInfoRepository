# State-of-Charge Estimation

**Summary:** Nine 2025 open-access studies generated their own test data on commercial cells (mostly 18650 formats, plus A123 prismatic/pouch cells) to develop and validate SOC estimators, split between equivalent-circuit-model-based Kalman filtering and data-driven machine learning. Thermal boundary conditions and cell temperature sensing are reported inconsistently, and temperature is rarely a modelled quantity.

## Synthesized Knowledge

**Model families and parameterization.** Model-based estimators use lumped equivalent circuit models without a thermal component. A fractional-order second-order RC model of the Samsung SDI INR18650-30Q, identified offline by the whale optimization algorithm from pulse-discharge data, was paired with a strong-tracking SVD-UKF for SOC and an EKF updating parameters and capacity; it reached mean SOC errors of 0.45% (UDDS), 0.51% (NEDC) and 0.35% (HWFET) (Source: [[cooperative-estimation-method-for-soc-and-soh-of-lithium-ion-batteries]]). An SVDUKF (micro time scale) with EKF (macro time scale) on a second-order RC model of the EVE INR18650/33V used an OCV–SOC–SOH 6th-order polynomial surface from HPPC tests at different aging stages, giving SOC RMSE 1.0531% (DST) and 1.5611% (FUDS) (Source: [[joint-estimation-of-soc-and-soh-based-on-kalman-filter-under-multi-tim]]). Online identification with forgetting-factor recursive least squares appears in two works: FFRLS plus a modified adaptive UKF for the A123 AMP20M1HD-A with an 8th-order polynomial OCV fit from HPPC (Source: [[hardware-in-the-loop-simulation-for-online-identification-of-lithium-i]]), and an improved adaptive FFRLS with a weighted multi-innovation Sage–Husa EKF, checked on FTP-75 data of the Samsung SDI INR18650-25R at 25 °C (MAE 0.0030, RMSE 0.0039) (Source: [[soc-estimation-for-lithium-ion-batteries-based-on-weighted-multi-innov]]). Data-driven estimators include an IDBO-optimized extreme learning machine for the LG 18650HG2 and Panasonic NCR18650PF (Source: [[a-combined-improved-dung-beetle-optimization-and-extreme-learning-mach]]), a SWATS-trained CNN-LSTM for the Panasonic NCR18650BD (Source: [[cnn-lstm-optimized-with-swats-for-accurate-state-of-charge-estimation]]), random-forest models for second-life Samsung SDI ICR18650-22P cells (Source: [[self-soc-estimation-for-second-life-lithium-ion-batteries]]) and for an A123 26 Ah pouch cell, where random forest (RMSE 0.0229) outperformed an ECM-based EKF (RMSE 0.0925) (Source: [[soc-estimation-for-a-lithium-ion-pouch-cell-using-machine-learning-und]]), and an XGBoost–random-forest stack with a dual-state Kalman filter on a 20S1P Samsung SDI INR18650-25R e-bike pack (Source: [[xgboost-random-forest-stacking-with-dual-state-kalman-filtering-for-re]]). Measured internal resistance as an extra input (Pearson correlation -0.960 with SOC) roughly halved the LA92 error for the NCR18650BD (MAE 0.017 vs. 0.036) (Source: [[cnn-lstm-optimized-with-swats-for-accurate-state-of-charge-estimation]]). Reference SOC is almost universally Coulomb counting / Ah integration (Source: [[a-combined-improved-dung-beetle-optimization-and-extreme-learning-mach]]; Source: [[self-soc-estimation-for-second-life-lithium-ion-batteries]]; Source: [[cooperative-estimation-method-for-soc-and-soh-of-lithium-ion-batteries]]).

**Ambient boundary conditions and temperature sensing.** Only some studies controlled temperature: thermal chambers at -20, 0, 10 and 25 °C (training set described as spanning -20 to 40 °C) for the LG/Panasonic cells, with cell temperature "recorded by the thermal chamber" and no sensor details (Source: [[a-combined-improved-dung-beetle-optimization-and-extreme-learning-mach]]); an ESPEC chamber at 0, 25 and 45 °C with 2 h soak and a TMP1117 sensor at 1 Hz (location not reported) (Source: [[cnn-lstm-optimized-with-swats-for-accurate-state-of-charge-estimation]]); a stated constant 25 °C without a reported control method (Source: [[soc-estimation-for-lithium-ion-batteries-based-on-weighted-multi-innov]]; Source: [[joint-estimation-of-soc-and-soh-based-on-kalman-filter-under-multi-tim]], where only the static capacity test temperature is stated); and multiple unspecified temperatures with pack BMS sensors at 1 Hz (Source: [[xgboost-random-forest-stacking-with-dual-state-kalman-filtering-for-re]]). Four studies report no ambient temperature and no cell temperature measurement at all (Source: [[cooperative-estimation-method-for-soc-and-soh-of-lithium-ion-batteries]]; Source: [[hardware-in-the-loop-simulation-for-online-identification-of-lithium-i]]; Source: [[self-soc-estimation-for-second-life-lithium-ion-batteries]]; Source: [[soc-estimation-for-a-lithium-ion-pouch-cell-using-machine-learning-und]]). No study reports a mechanical boundary condition.

**Temperature dependence of estimation accuracy — contradictory findings.** The IDBO-ELM reports nearly temperature-independent UDDS RMSE for the LG 18650HG2 (0.0135, 0.0120, 0.0128 at -20, 0 and 25 °C) (Source: [[a-combined-improved-dung-beetle-optimization-and-extreme-learning-mach]]). In contrast, the CNN-LSTM exceeded 10% maximum error at 0 °C on the public CALCE data used for benchmarking (Source: [[cnn-lstm-optimized-with-swats-for-accurate-state-of-charge-estimation]]), and on the INR18650-25R pack HEAD-KF error rose only modestly at the lower temperature bound while LSTM error grew more strongly and RC-EKF drift was attributed to temperature-dependent impedance violating constant-parameter assumptions (Source: [[xgboost-random-forest-stacking-with-dual-state-kalman-filtering-for-re]]). Thus whether low temperature substantially degrades SOC accuracy differs by study and method; the conditions (cell, dataset, temperature set) are not comparable.

**Limitations and deployment — contradictory findings.** Recurring limitations are larger errors at low SOC (below 0.1 SOC due to polarization (Source: [[a-combined-improved-dung-beetle-optimization-and-extreme-learning-mach]]); SOH and voltage errors increasing below 20% SOC (Source: [[joint-estimation-of-soc-and-soh-based-on-kalman-filter-under-multi-tim]])), unconsidered aging/SOH coupling (Source: [[a-combined-improved-dung-beetle-optimization-and-extreme-learning-mach]]; Source: [[soc-estimation-for-lithium-ion-batteries-based-on-weighted-multi-innov]]), validation restricted to constant 25 °C laboratory profiles (Source: [[soc-estimation-for-lithium-ion-batteries-based-on-weighted-multi-innov]]), and hardware noise raising errors in real-time settings (HIL vs. simulation (Source: [[hardware-in-the-loop-simulation-for-online-identification-of-lithium-i]]); real-time SOC RMSE 1.8% vs. 0.85% offline (Source: [[self-soc-estimation-for-second-life-lithium-ion-batteries]])). On real-time feasibility of machine learning the papers disagree: one states ML needs significant computing resources and training data, posing obstacles for real-time deployment (Source: [[soc-estimation-for-a-lithium-ion-pouch-cell-using-machine-learning-und]]), whereas others deploy random-forest estimators on a Raspberry Pi (Source: [[self-soc-estimation-for-second-life-lithium-ion-batteries]]) including an XGBoost–RF ensemble at about 6 ms per update (Source: [[xgboost-random-forest-stacking-with-dual-state-kalman-filtering-for-re]]).

## Literature Mentions

- [[a-combined-improved-dung-beetle-optimization-and-extreme-learning-mach]]: IDBO-ELM on LG 18650HG2 and Panasonic NCR18650PF drive cycles at -20 to 25 °C (training to 40 °C), MAE/RMSE around 1.4%.
- [[cnn-lstm-optimized-with-swats-for-accurate-state-of-charge-estimation]]: SWATS CNN-LSTM for Panasonic NCR18650BD at 0/25/45 °C; internal resistance input halves LA92 error.
- [[cooperative-estimation-method-for-soc-and-soh-of-lithium-ion-batteries]]: Fractional-order RC model with SVD-UKF/EKF for Samsung SDI INR18650-30Q, mean SOC error 0.35–0.51%; temperature not reported.
- [[hardware-in-the-loop-simulation-for-online-identification-of-lithium-i]]: FFRLS + modified adaptive UKF for A123 AMP20M1HD-A, validated in simulation, 0.5C discharge and HIL; temperature not reported.
- [[joint-estimation-of-soc-and-soh-based-on-kalman-filter-under-multi-tim]]: Multi-time-scale SVDUKF-EKF for EVE INR18650/33V with OCV–SOC–SOH surface; SOC RMSE 1.05% (DST), 1.56% (FUDS).
- [[self-soc-estimation-for-second-life-lithium-ion-batteries]]: Two-layer random forest for second-life Samsung SDI ICR18650-22P, SOC RMSE 0.85% offline, 1.8% real-time on Raspberry Pi.
- [[soc-estimation-for-a-lithium-ion-pouch-cell-using-machine-learning-und]]: Random forest beats EKF and Coulomb counting on A123 26 Ah pouch cell across four load profiles (RMSE 0.0229).
- [[soc-estimation-for-lithium-ion-batteries-based-on-weighted-multi-innov]]: IAFFRLS + WMISAEKF checked on Samsung SDI INR18650-25R FTP-75 data at 25 °C (RMSE 0.0039).
- [[xgboost-random-forest-stacking-with-dual-state-kalman-filtering-for-re]]: XGBoost–RF stacking with dual-state KF on a 20S1P Samsung SDI INR18650-25R e-bike pack, ~6 ms on Raspberry Pi 4.
