# Effects of Different Safety Vent Bursting Pressures on Lithium-Ion Battery Thermal Runaway Process and Reaction Product Compositions

## General Analysis

**Core Thesis:** The paper investigates how safety vent bursting pressures of 1, 2 and 3 MPa affect the thermal runaway process and reaction products of Samsung SDI INR18650-30Q NMC622/graphite cells. The original vent is replaced by aluminium foil discs in a self-developed canister, cells at 100% SOC are heated at 5 °C/min in a nitrogen-filled 8 L vessel, and vented gases and solid residues are analysed. Higher bursting pressure delays venting, increases gas volume (5.3 to 6.5 L) and H2 content, and changes the solid product composition.

**Methodological Focus**
- Testing Mode: Heating-triggered thermal runaway (abuse) tests in a sealed pressure vessel; gas chromatography (TCD/FID); XRD and SEM-EDS of solid products
- Operating Conditions: 100% SOC after five conditioning cycles; heating at 5 °C/min in nitrogen at atmospheric pressure (>99% N2); vent bursting pressures of 1, 2 and 3 MPa
- Degradation Markers: None

## Keyword Context

- [[thermal-runaway|Thermal Runaway]]: Thermal runaway of the INR18650-30Q is triggered by heating, and its timing, vessel pressure/temperature and gas production are compared across vent bursting pressures.
- Safety Vent: The cell safety vent is removed and replaced by 4, 8 or 12 layers of aluminium foil to set bursting pressures of 1, 2 and 3 MPa.
- Vent Gas Analysis: Vented gases are collected in sampling bags and analysed by gas chromatography, showing H2 rising from 23.92% to 28.81% with increasing bursting pressure.
