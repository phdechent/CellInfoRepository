# Raman Spectroscopy of Practical LIB Cathodes: A Study of Humidity-Induced Degradation

## General Analysis

**Core Thesis:** The study defines an ex situ Raman spectroscopy pipeline for practical cathodes harvested from commercial cells, using LFP cathodes from pristine A123 ANR26650M1B cells (and NMC-LMO from Sony US18650V3). Cathodes opened in an argon glove-box (reference) are compared with cathodes opened in humid laboratory air to identify structural, morphological and capacity changes caused by humidity exposure.

**Methodological Focus**
- Testing Mode: Cell disassembly and cathode harvesting; confocal Raman spectroscopy (632.8 nm He-Ne laser, D2 filter); SEM/EDS; coin-cell galvanostatic cycling at C/10
- Operating Conditions: Humidity exposure by opening cells in air at ca. 25 °C and ca. 50% humidity; LFP coin cells cycled at C/10 between 2.5 and 3.65 V
- Degradation Markers: Coin-cell capacity fade; blue shift of carbon D and G Raman bands; 957 cm−1 shoulder of delithiated LFP; etched, spheroidized particle morphology

## Keyword Context

- Raman Spectroscopy: Ex situ confocal Raman spectroscopy is the central technique used to detect structural changes of pristine versus humidity-exposed cathodes harvested from the target cell.
- [[post-mortem-analysis|Post-Mortem Analysis]]: Pristine commercial cells were discharged and opened either in an argon glove-box or in humid air to harvest reference and humidity-damaged cathodes.
- [[capacity-degradation|Capacity Degradation]]: Coin cells built from humidity-exposed cathodes were cycled at C/10 to associate capacity losses with humidity damage, with LFP showing slower fade than NCM-LMO.

## Cell Experiment and Modelling Review

### Q1: Study Purpose and Approach

**Answer:** The study aims to establish Raman spectroscopy, complemented by SEM, EDS and coin-cell tests, for detecting humidity-induced degradation in practical LFP cathodes harvested from the A123 ANR26650M1B cell; approach: Experimental

> Evidence: "LFP cathodes were obtained from A123 ANR26650M1B, 26650 cylindrical format, 2300 mAh, 50 A, 3.3 V"

### Q2: Experimental Purpose and Protocol

**Answer:** Purpose: compare reference (glove-box opened, R-LFP) and humidity-exposed (air opened, H-LFP) cathodes and link structural changes to capacity loss; tests: disassembly after discharge to the minimum safe cut-off voltage, confocal Raman spectroscopy with optical-density calibration, SEM and EDS, CR2032 coin-cell formation (3 CC cycles at C/10) and capacity-fade cycling at C/10 in 2.5–3.65 V for LFP; data origin: Own experiments

> Evidence: "Cathodes without and with humidity exposure were also subjected to cycling in coin cells, to assess the residual capacity and the capacity fade rate."
