# Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy

**Summary:** Across the 2025 open-access papers, EIS on commercial cells is used for dataset characterization at several temperatures and SOCs, for ECM parameterization (including fractional-order and electrochemical-thermal models), for aging and second-life tracking, for online sensor design, for module-level measurement correction, and for thermal-runaway early warning. Frequency ranges, SOC points, excitation amplitudes and temperature control differ strongly between studies, and several studies do not report the test temperature at all.

## Synthesized Knowledge

**Protocols and boundary conditions.** Reported frequency windows vary widely: 0.01 Hz–3 kHz at 20/40/60/80% SOC for the CALB L148N58A prismatic cell (Source: [[a-dataset-for-large-prismatic-lithium-ion-battery-cells-calb-l148n58a]]), 10 mHz–10 kHz at 20/50/80% SoC for the Molicel INR-21700-P42A (Source: [[high-power-lithium-ion-battery-characterization-dataset-for-stochastic]]), 0.1 Hz–10 kHz with 500 mA amplitude at 100/80/50/20/0% SOC for the LG INR21700 M50T (Source: [[utilization-of-battery-analysis-methodologies-for-parametrization-and]]), 0.1 Hz–1 kHz (41 points) at 50% SOC for the Samsung INR21700-40T (Source: [[development-of-a-correction-algorithm-for-structural-elements-to-enhan]]), 250 kHz–10 mHz at five cell voltages (4.2–2.8 V) for the Panasonic-Sanyo NCR18650GA (Source: [[experimental-investigation-of-the-impact-of-high-frequency-alternating]]), 1 Hz–1.6 kHz with 50 mA amplitude for the Samsung INR21700-50E (Source: [[impedance-based-thermal-runaway-early-detection-methodology-for-lithiu]]), and a 1 A amplitude, 45-point sweep up to 2000 Hz for the Samsung INR18650-35E (Source: [[impact-of-first-life-usage-on-second-life-performance-of-lithium-ion-b]]). Ambient control is equally heterogeneous: thermal chambers at 10/25/40 °C for the CALB cell with no stated tolerance (Source: [[a-dataset-for-large-prismatic-lithium-ion-battery-cells-calb-l148n58a]]); 5/25/40 °C for the Molicel P42A, where about 20% of the 5 °C EIS tests actually recorded 8–10 °C before chamber regulation was fixed (Source: [[high-power-lithium-ion-battery-characterization-dataset-for-stochastic]]); 25 °C to eliminate temperature-induced impedance changes for the INR21700-40T modules (Source: [[development-of-a-correction-algorithm-for-structural-elements-to-enhan]]); 25 °C ± 2 °C characterization for the NCR18650GA (Source: [[experimental-investigation-of-the-impact-of-high-frequency-alternating]]); a 20 °C setpoint with unquantified variation for the INR18650-35E (Source: [[impact-of-first-life-usage-on-second-life-performance-of-lithium-ion-b]]); and a 20 °C reference temperature for the M50T parameters (Source: [[utilization-of-battery-analysis-methodologies-for-parametrization-and]]). The two Samsung ICR18650-26J studies measured a fully charged cell with a Hioki IM3590 but report no temperature, mechanical constraint or cell temperature sensing (Source: [[an-integrated-co-simulation-framework-for-the-design-analysis-and-perf]]; Source: [[multiband-multisine-excitation-signal-for-online-impedance-spectroscop]]). Mechanical constraint is rarely described beyond fixtures such as a holding/contacting mandrel (Source: [[utilization-of-battery-analysis-methodologies-for-parametrization-and]]) or 18650 holders with welded joints and 4-probe contacts (Source: [[impact-of-first-life-usage-on-second-life-performance-of-lithium-ion-b]]).

**Temperature dependence of impedance.** For the Molicel P42A, EIS curves shrink with increasing temperature, with the largest semicircles at 5 °C (Source: [[high-power-lithium-ion-battery-characterization-dataset-for-stochastic]]); for the CALB L148N58A, HPPC-derived ohmic resistance is higher at lower temperature (Source: [[a-dataset-for-large-prismatic-lithium-ion-battery-cells-calb-l148n58a]]). Over a much wider range (25 °C to about 120 °C in ARC), the Samsung INR21700-50E impedance first decreased with temperature and then reversed and increased from approximately 90 °C; at 400.15 Hz the phase was more temperature-dependent and the magnitude more SoC-dependent, which was exploited for thermal-runaway warnings about 15 h (slow) and 6–12 min (fast abuse) ahead (Source: [[impedance-based-thermal-runaway-early-detection-methodology-for-lithiu]]). This non-monotonic behaviour above ~90 °C lies outside the ranges covered by the other studies, so the monotonic "impedance shrinks with temperature" observation should be read as valid only for the moderate ranges tested (5–40 °C). A related contradiction concerns capacity: the CALB median C/20 capacity rises monotonically from 10 to 40 °C (Source: [[a-dataset-for-large-prismatic-lithium-ion-battery-cells-calb-l148n58a]]), whereas the Molicel P42A shows the highest average capacity at 25 °C (about 3.9 Ah) and lower values at both 5 °C and 40 °C (about 3.8 Ah) (Source: [[high-power-lithium-ion-battery-characterization-dataset-for-stochastic]]).

**EIS for modelling and parameterization.** For the M50T, EIS spectra of four cells at five SOC points were averaged and fitted to an ECM (Rs, two RC elements, RC sub-element, Warburg) with polynomial SOC dependence and a linear temperature coefficient referenced to 20 °C, coupled to a 2D thermal network; validation from −20 to 40 °C gave ~1.5% (<50 mV) mean discharge voltage deviation, but current dependence had to be added empirically and aging was not measured (Source: [[utilization-of-battery-analysis-methodologies-for-parametrization-and]]). For the ICR18650-26J, a modified Randles ECM with a CPE (approximated by a Cauer I ladder within 1% over 4–120 Hz) served as the lumped cell model in a Cadence–Matlab co-simulation; hardware refinement reduced RMS deviation from 18.5/17.8 mΩ to 0.59/0.86 mΩ (real/imaginary), with connection impedances unmodelled and valid only for a fully charged cell (Source: [[an-integrated-co-simulation-framework-for-the-design-analysis-and-perf]]). The same fractional-order ECM supported a multiband multisine excitation that reduced magnitude RMSE from 7.30 mΩ to 0.40 mΩ in [1–100] Hz simulation (Source: [[multiband-multisine-excitation-signal-for-online-impedance-spectroscop]]). For the NCR18650GA, EIS characterized an ECM (series inductance, R_L1||L1, R0, two RC elements, Warburg) used to estimate AC-heater currents, with resistances and inductance assumed constant (Source: [[experimental-investigation-of-the-impact-of-high-frequency-alternating]]).

**Module effects and aging.** Nickel bus plates in 2P–4P INR21700-40T modules contributed about 15–25% of total impedance; a geometry-based correction (3.7 nH/mm inductance) reduced RMSE from 1.18–2.65 mΩ to 0.10–0.14 mΩ, validated only at 25 °C, 50% SOC and fresh cells (Source: [[development-of-a-correction-algorithm-for-structural-elements-to-enhan]]). For aging, EIS localized the extra degradation of periodically AC-heated NCR18650GA cells mainly in the ohmic region (ΔR_AC) (Source: [[experimental-investigation-of-the-impact-of-high-frequency-alternating]]), and ACIR at 1000 Hz was tracked alongside DCIR through first and second life of INR18650-35E cells (Source: [[impact-of-first-life-usage-on-second-life-performance-of-lithium-ion-b]]).

## Literature Mentions

- [[a-dataset-for-large-prismatic-lithium-ion-battery-cells-calb-l148n58a]]: EIS (0.01 Hz–3 kHz, four SOCs) on eleven CALB L148N58A prismatic cells at 10/25/40 °C for a cell-to-cell variation dataset.
- [[an-integrated-co-simulation-framework-for-the-design-analysis-and-perf]]: Hioki EIS on a fully charged Samsung ICR18650-26J parameterizes a CPE-based Randles ECM in a co-simulation of an online EIS sensor.
- [[development-of-a-correction-algorithm-for-structural-elements-to-enhan]]: Bus plate artifacts in EIS of 2P–4P Samsung INR21700-40T modules corrected geometrically at 25 °C, 50% SOC.
- [[experimental-investigation-of-the-impact-of-high-frequency-alternating]]: EIS at five voltages shows AC-heating-related aging of Panasonic-Sanyo NCR18650GA cells lies mainly in ohmic resistance.
- [[high-power-lithium-ion-battery-characterization-dataset-for-stochastic]]: EIS of 12 Molicel INR-21700-P42A cells at 5/25/40 °C and three SoCs; spectra shrink with temperature, R0 distribution reported.
- [[impact-of-first-life-usage-on-second-life-performance-of-lithium-ion-b]]: EIS/ACIR at 1000 Hz tracked through first and second life of retired Samsung INR18650-35E cells at a 20 °C setpoint.
- [[impedance-based-thermal-runaway-early-detection-methodology-for-lithiu]]: Single-frequency (400.15 Hz) EIS phase/magnitude used for thermal-runaway early warning of Samsung INR21700-50E cells in ARC and abuse tests.
- [[multiband-multisine-excitation-signal-for-online-impedance-spectroscop]]: Fractional-order ECM of a fully charged Samsung ICR18650-26J used to show multiband multisine excitation lowers online EIS error.
- [[utilization-of-battery-analysis-methodologies-for-parametrization-and]]: Own EIS and OCV data parameterize an ECM coupled to a thermal network for the LG INR21700 M50T, validated from −20 to 40 °C.
